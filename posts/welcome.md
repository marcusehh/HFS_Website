UCL Hedge Fund Society was established in 2022 with a clear mission: to prepare students for careers in finance through a hands-on, practical approach.

We combine investment training with application support, giving members the skills and experience they need to stand out in an increasingly competitive industry. Whether you're interested in equities research, macro trading, or quantitative strategies, HFS provides a platform to learn, practise and connect.

Our programmes — the Equity Paper Fund, LUSIF and our Fundamental Edge partnership — offer real exposure to the investment process, from idea generation through to portfolio construction.

We look forward to sharing our research and insights here.
